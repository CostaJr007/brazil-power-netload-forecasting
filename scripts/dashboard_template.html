What we learnedDuring the Nov 3–7 heat wave the operator's bias flipped sign; the 28-day bias correction kept removing the old bias and D+1 lost to the operator. D+2 still won by about 20%. Nothing was retuned: adjusting to one bad week would be overfitting.

Each forecast was rebuilt with only the data available at 10 pm the night before: data cut at that time, archived weather, frozen recipes. Actuals are today's ONS balance; the most recent day is still preliminary.
